About us

Spoke & Sprocket started in 2014 in a garage with one work stand and a lot of spare tubes. Today we are a team of four mechanics, and we still treat every bike like it is our own.

Whether it is a ten-year-old school bike or a carbon road machine, you get the same careful work and a plain-English explanation of what we did.
